Coconut Climbing & Home Care

Trained climbers and cleaning crews for coconut trees, tanks, wells and compounds.

Your needs, KKC Group is always with you.

Our trained climbers use safety harnesses and proper equipment to harvest coconuts and trim palms, so your trees stay healthy and your compound stays safe.

Beyond the trees, the same team cleans homes and surroundings, scrubs and disinfects water tanks, cuts and clears unwanted trees, and cleans wells. One call covers the whole property.

What is included

  • Coconut climbing and harvesting
  • House and surroundings cleaning
  • Water tank cleaning
  • Tree cutting and well cleaning
