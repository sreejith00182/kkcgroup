About KKC Group

Reliable, professional and trusted.

Our story

KKC Group started with a simple idea: people in Kozhikode should be able to call one number for the everyday help they need. What began with home and tree care grew, step by step, into AC service, manpower, courier, cabs, real estate, readymade walls and holiday stays.

Today our teams work across Kerala. We have kept the first promise: honest work, fair prices and a phone that gets answered.
